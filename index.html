ROBOCUS

Robot fights, schemes and bets for 4 teams.

  • How to play ROBOCUS 2.0: the short rulebook for friends (you have played Spartacus), with the robots in an interactive clip.
  • Tabletop prototype: solo vs 3 AI teams, or 🌐 Online with friends.
  • Tabletop prototype 2.0: ROBOCUS 2.0, work in progress: physics fights, damage that stays, crew repairs. Separate from the game above.
  • Draft Arena: test mode, separate from the games: online 1v1, 2v2 or free-for-all. Draft a robot and upgrades each round, watch the fight, collect points.
  • 2.0 Damage Lab and 2.0 Lineup Arena: test benches for the 2.0 fights.
  • Scrapyard Dominus: the older full round loop.
  • Arena sandbox: build robots and watch fights.
