ROBOCUS 3D Arena

A sketch of a 3D look on top of the normal 2.0 fight engine. Same robots, same physics and same seeds as the Lineup Arena; only the drawing is new. Drag the arena to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom.

    • What is real here: every part is the engine's own shape, pulled up into 3D, so armour tearing off, wheels flying and dents all come from the fight itself.
    • What is acted out: the engine is flat, so height, flips and tilts are animated from what it already tracks (in the air, lifted by a wedge or fork, upside down).
    • Not drawn yet: legs, overhead axes and some level 4 arms are plain blocks; lasers and drone beams are missing.
