    Matchup overview

    The 2.0 lineup

    • Levels. 9 starters (level 0) and 16 market robots in levels 1-3. Each robot card has a power grade: its weapon damage and every part's HP times that number (x0.76 to x1.21). In 3,600 test fights each level beat the level below about 60-62% of the time, two levels up 77%, three levels 91%.
    • Parts (22 cards, 18 types). Drones, modules and stat cards. A module part replaces the robot's own module. The % in the picker is how often a robot with that part beats robots one level up (stock: about 38%).
    • Legendaries (3). Prototype core, Field hospital and the reworked Quantum shield (phases out on the first real hit, recharges in 8 s). The % is against robots two levels up (stock: about 25%).
    • Tuning chips (3). Matador, Wall Bait and Pester: a fighting routine for light robots, one per robot. The % is a level 1 robot with the chip against level 3 (stock: about 23%). From level 2 up a chip can glitch (15 / 30 / 45% chance of a 1 s glitch every 4 s).
    • Arenas (5). Open floor, Floor saws, Killbox, the Cage (6 x 6 m, walls 1 m in) and the Ice rink (grip x0.4; legs keep their footing). Pick one under the arena.
    • Same physics as the Damage Lab. Parts tear off, robots get flipped, the arena strikes back. Same seed, same fight.
