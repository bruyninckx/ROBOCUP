Robot Arena Sandbox

Who beats whom (1 vs 1)

Row against column, 200 simulated fights each. Click a cell to load that duel.

  • With 3 or 4 robots each one shoots its nearest enemy and switches when another gets clearly closer. Friendly fire is on: stray bullets, mortar splash (the shooter's own too) and the laser hurt teammates. Robots hold fire when a teammate is right in the line of fire, but spread and movement still cause accidents. The laser beam goes through every robot in its path.
  • Every robot spends 14 points over 7 stats (0–4 each), then picks a gun calibre, a preferred range, and a weapon.
  • Rapid guns: many small bullets, quick turret. Armour removes a flat amount from every hit, so it shrugs off rapid fire.
  • Heavy guns: big slow shells and a slow turret that needs precise aim. Armour and health add weight, which costs speed.
  • Modules (one per robot) are the surprise cards: Shield, Flipper, Decoy, Mines, Repair Drone, EMP, Self-Destruct, Smoke. Tick "Hide modules" to see them the way an opponent would: just a ? until the module reveals itself.
  • Laser: ignores armour, short range, has to keep its beam on the target. Mortar: lobbed shell with splash, can't fire up close.
