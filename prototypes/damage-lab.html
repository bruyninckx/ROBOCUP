    How the damage works

    • Outer parts are physical. Armour plates, wheels, front tools and the spinner bar are real pieces bolted on. When one runs out of HP it tears off with its own speed and spin and stays in the arena as debris that robots bump into, push around or knock into the pit.
    • Inner parts get hit through the hull. Battery, radio, wheel motors and the weapon motor sit inside. A hit on the bare chassis passes part of its damage to the closest one; armour only lets big hits through. Battery dead or radio dead = KO.
    • Hits come from physics. Damage follows the impulse of each collision: a spun-up bar or a slam into spiked walls hurts, a slow push does nothing. Sharp parts (bar, saws, spikes) hit harder, the wedge and drum shrug things off.
    • Broken parts change how a robot drives. A bent wheel wobbles and loses grip, a dead motor leaves a wheel freewheeling, a missing wheel makes that corner scrape and pull, a big hit can jam a motor on so the robot spins in circles. Lose all drive for 6 s, or limp about crippled without real movement for 12 s, and you are counted out.
    • Heat and fire. Motors heat up when they push without moving (hot-wound ones twice as fast) and cut power above 85°. Flames and the laser cook parts; a damaged or hot battery can catch fire.
    • Flips. Drums and flippers throw robots. Robots with a turret, axe or crusher on top land helpless on their back unless they can self-right (flipper, axe) or bash a wall to roll back. Hard landings damage wheels.
    • Bumpers. Every robot picks one shell for its armour plates: steel (tough, heavy), rubber (shrugs off rams and punches, blades cut through), spring (throws attackers back across the arena), spiked (hurts whoever rams it) or fibreglass (very light, shatters in a shower of shards).
    • Arcade rules. Weapon hits throw robots around and parts fly far. Crates drop in now and then: nitro, overcharge (damage ×1.8), repair and shield. Every 15-20 s the arena strikes: walls close in, floor bumpers launch robots, or the ceiling hammer comes down on a red circle. Big hits fill the hype bar; full hype means 6 s of rage (harder hits, faster). The last 30 s are sudden death: the arena strikes every 7 s.
    • 2v2 and free-for-all. Four robots in the pit. 2v2: robots 1 & 3 (Steel) against 2 & 4 (Rust), friendly fire does 35%. FFA: one robot per corner, last one standing. Each robot picks its own target: the closest, a wounded one, whoever just hit it, or (2v2) whoever is beating up its teammate. Knockouts are credited to the last robot that hit. Time: judges add damage, parts ripped off, knockouts and survival.
    • Same seed, same fight. The sim is deterministic, so Replay shows exactly the same fight again. Time limit 2:00, then the judges score damage, parts ripped off and aggression.
