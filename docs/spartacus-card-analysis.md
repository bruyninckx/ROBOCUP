# Spartacus card analysis → ROBOCUS (2026-11-01)

Copied from the Claude project notes (`spartacus-card-analysis.md`). Full doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/R4pMF4CuurAnmz4zE16siy
Source: Kev's photos of the complete physical set (kept locally, not in the repo). Includes some *Serpents & the Wolf* expansion cards (↑/↓ arrow = need more/less influence than target).

## Card anatomy
- Intrigue: top-left = influence needed (supporters can add theirs; reactions can't be supported); bottom coin = cash-in gold. Blue Scheme, red Reaction, green Guard (discard + roll 4–6 to foil a scheme on you, or place as asset).
- Market: ATK/DEF/SPD dice, gold value 1–5, one ability line (some tagged Intrigue/Market = off-arena). White border = starting card.

## Intrigue deck (~165 cards, 59 unique read), families
Economy 10, Influence up 15, Influence down 11 (some gated above the win score, need backing), Asset attacks 7, Market tricks 3, Arena tricks 2, Defence 10 (~35 Guard copies; one card foils anything), Coercion 1 (forces support).

## Market deck
Gladiators: stat line + one rule-break. Slaves 1/1/1 with off-arena powers (extra gold, spying, card draw, guard re-rolls). Equipment = mostly "exhaust: once per fight" abilities.

## Proposed ROBOCUS adoption
- Four rules: fame gate + backing on every card; cash-in value; Guards → Security Bot (card or garage asset); Exhaust/Ready for robots and crew.
- Families renamed: Contracts, Publicity, Smear, Sabotage, Corporate, Match-fixing, Security, Leverage.
- Market: gladiator → robot with a "quirk"; slave → crew role; equipment → parts/modules.
- Upgrade rule: permanent upgrades (weapons, armour, modules, helpers, stat upgrades, legendary parts) = Market cards; one-fight boosts (Secret Upgrade, Overclock) = Intrigue cards. Slots: 1 weapon, 1 armour, 1 module, up to 3 helpers. Stat upgrades add progression Spartacus lacks → cap per robot + limited upgrade cards to avoid snowballing.
- Proposed 50-card Market deck; starter robots/mechanics outside the deck (like white-border cards).
- Proposed Intrigue deck: Spartacus counts, 65 unique, ~165 with copies (fewer duplicate Security Bots).
- Difficulty: card/economy layer easy; backing, reaction windows, trading, threshold rescaling (5 → 3 robots) medium; robot quirks (sim code + watchdog), AI deal-making hard; dice/hex rules dropped.

## Decisions (Kev)
- 2026-11-01: Crew (mechanics) stay off-arena; helper drones remain a separate thing.
- 2026-11-01: Parts are OPEN (like Spartacus), not hidden. Surprise comes from Intrigue cards. The bookmaker sees parts too.
- 2026-11-01: Market deck ≈ 50 different cards, stay close to Spartacus sizes, include upgrade cards.
