# Intrigue deck (v1 + 5 Nov additions + 7 Nov trim)

57 different cards, 130 copies (7 Nov trim and 8 Nov card pass, Kev: was 75 cards / 188 copies), in 7 families: schemes and blockers only. Gate = fame needed (▲ more fame than the target, ▼ less); cash-in = credits when discarded for money. The live fight cards and Swap the Entry are in the **Auction deck** (below), not in the Intrigue deck. Event cards make a fight bigger (target: about 1 fight in 4). Full rules: `docs/rulebook.md`.

## Auction deck (7 cards, 16 copies; Kev, 7 Nov)

Not dealt to hands. In every fight's **Bets step** 2 cards are turned up and auctioned while everyone bets:

- **Robot bets are public** and update live as anyone clicks. Changing your bet un-locks everyone who already locked, so they can react. No timer.
- **Card bids are sealed.** A 🔥 shows a card is wanted (3+ credits bid on it in total, or 2+ teams bidding), not how much or by whom.
- Everyone locks bet and card bids together. Then the bets are paid, then the cards are resolved, highest bid first.
- The highest bid wins and pays its bid (minimum 1 credit). **One card per team per fight.** A tie goes to the team with less fame; with equal fame nobody gets it.
- A won card is for **this fight only**: unused at the end of the fight, it is discarded. Cards nobody bids on are discarded. The deck reshuffles when it runs out.
- No fame gates (you paid for it). The bookie's odds ignore the cards on purpose.
- AI teams bid only when they fight or have a bet on the fight, more when more is riding on it.
