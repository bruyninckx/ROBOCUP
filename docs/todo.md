## Decisions settled by Kev, 5 Nov (interview 1: rules)
- Fight size: option D. Duels are the normal fight; bigger formats are events, ~1 in 4 fights.
- Weapon swaps: a bought weapon can replace the signature weapon; old one stays as a spare (re-equip or sell to bank); swap removes the quirk FOR GOOD; warning shown before confirming. Railgun stays on the Railgunner.
- Market deck stays 50. Live combat cards (6) → Intrigue, Match-fixing family.
- Squadron leaders: out for now. Drive types: build real movement (spinner/tank/wheels), outside the watchdog.
- Auction: lots one at a time; double tie with nobody adding → lower fame wins, shown as a warning in game; Sovereign Foundry wins ties outright.
- Modules: open like all parts.
- Intrigue: 28 Security Bots; keep both House Robot and Grand Final; Joint Venture = prisoner's dilemma.
- Backing, reactions, cash-in, Ready/Exhausted: copy Spartacus 1:1, adjust after playtests.
- Part slots: 1 weapon, 1 armour, 1 module, up to 3 helpers. Every upgrade adds strain. Helper upkeep 1 credit per 2 helpers. Breakable: legendaries + value 4–5 parts; engineers lower the chance.
- Bookie odds: 5 fuzzy bands.
