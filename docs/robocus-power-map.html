What an upgrade is worth

Every part on every robot against every robot a level up, with and without the part on the same seeds (11,424 fights a round, after levelling on 15 Nov). The bar is how many levels the part adds: 1.0 means a robot with it beats the level above as often as it beats its own level. Good to about ±0.15 overall, ±0.3 for one robot group.

Build calculator

Pick a robot and its upgrades (2 part slots and a legendary slot, like the game). It adds the measured value of each part on a robot of that level (half that level group's number, half the all-robot one, to smooth the noise), swaps in the measured number for an epic combo or a great or poor fit, and turns the result into a win chance against a robot of each level as it comes. Parts add up evenly (two parts ≈ twice one; see the tile above).

In the game: one idea

Show upgrades in the same unit as levels, so players read a build at a glance and see why the odds move. Stars for the robot's level, quarter pips for what its upgrades add. The pips come from the table above, the same numbers the bookie uses.
